# Mixscribe

**Your set, written down.** A DJ companion that listens, identifies and keeps track — so that after the live, everything is ready.

> 🚧 **Early days.** Mixscribe is being designed. There is no usable release yet — this page describes where it's going.

## What it will do

- **After the live** — a reliable, timestamped tracklist of your set, ready to paste into a YouTube description (`00:00 Artist – Title`), with streaming links. Plus the material for your post-set promo.
- **Track recognition** — identify the tracks of older sets that have no play history, and the tracks you hear while raving.
- **During the live** *(still being explored)* — set recording and a live tracklist.

Built by a DJ, for DJs — with VR raves (VRChat) in mind, but not limited to them.

## Principles

- **Your own sets first.** Mixscribe is meant to document *your* music. It is not a tool to rip the full tracklists of other DJs' sets without their consent.
- **Light on the services it relies on.** Recognition requests stay occasional and modest.
- **Open source**, under the GPL-3.0.
